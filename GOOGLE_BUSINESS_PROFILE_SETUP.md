## Step 8: Verification

Google will ask you to verify your business. Choose the best method:

### Option 1: Phone Verification (Fastest)
- Google will call the number you provided
- Enter the verification code when prompted

### Option 2: Email Verification
- Google sends a verification code to your email
- Enter the code in your Business Profile

### Option 3: Postcard (If address required)
- Google sends a postcard to your business address
- Takes 1-2 weeks
- Enter the code when you receive it


---
