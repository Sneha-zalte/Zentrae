Brand name searches require **time and authority**. Google needs:

1. **Brand Recognition** - Multiple signals that "zentrae" is a recognized brand
2. **Backlinks** - Other websites mentioning and linking to you
3. **Social Signals** - Social media presence with your brand name
4. **Search Volume** - People actually searching for "zentrae"
5. **Time** - Can take 1-3 months for brand searches to rank
